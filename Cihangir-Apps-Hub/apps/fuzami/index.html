Your pet's story belongs to you.

No Fuzami account is required. Your active pet records are stored on your device, with optional Smart Backup recovery points in your private iCloud app data. Fuzami has no ads and does not sell your personal information.

Learn about Privacy

A safer way to keep their story.

When you turn on Smart Backup, Fuzami creates versioned recovery points in your private iCloud app data. Recovery points can include records, routes, small photo previews, and health documents when space allows. Full-quality photos and videos are not duplicated into the backup.

Smart Backup is optional and provides backup and restore rather than live synchronization between devices. You can also export a backup file to keep in Files, another cloud service, or on a computer.
