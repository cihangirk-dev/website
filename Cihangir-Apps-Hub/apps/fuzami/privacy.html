Privacy Policy for Fuzami

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Fuzami (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Fuzami app for iPhone and iPad, visit the Fuzami promotional website, or contact us for support.

1. Our approach to privacy

Fuzami does not require a Fuzami account. We do not operate a separate server that receives your pet profiles, photos, notes, health records, health documents, or saved walk routes.

Your app records are stored on your device and may synchronize through Apple's CloudKit in your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Fuzami does not contain advertising and does not use your information for advertising tracking.

2. Information you choose to store in the app

Depending on the features you use, Fuzami may process the following information on your device and through your private iCloud database:

  • Pet profile information, such as a pet's name, species, breed, birth date, adoption date, profile photo, allergies, diet, ideal weight, and microchip identifier.
  • Daily moments, including photos, mood selections, notes, dates, and favorite status.
  • Health and care information, including vaccinations, parasite treatments, veterinarian visits, medications, surgeries, grooming, symptoms, lab results, dental care, insurance records, veterinarian names, notes, care schedules, dosage information, medication instructions, stock information, and completion history.
  • Weight records.
  • Health documents and attachments, such as PDFs, images, text files, CSV files, and Word documents.
  • Walk information, including start and end times, duration, distance, speed, and precise route points collected while a walk is active.
  • Generated cards, recap information, and time-lapse entries.
  • App settings, such as units, theme, reminder times, selected pet, and widget selection.

Please do not enter personal or sensitive information about other people unless you are authorized to do so and it is necessary for your use of the app.

3. Local storage and iCloud

Fuzami uses protected local app storage. If you are signed in to iCloud and CloudKit is available, app records, small image previews, and health-document data may synchronize through the app's private CloudKit database so that records can remain available across your devices.

Fuzami does not upload full original photo files to CloudKit. Original photos remain in your Photos library or in Fuzami's private local app storage. Small previews may synchronize through iCloud. Health-document data may also synchronize because those documents are intended to remain available across your devices.

Apple provides and controls iCloud and CloudKit. Your use of those services is also subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy.

4. Device permissions

Fuzami may request the following permissions when you choose to use a related feature:

  • Camera: To take pet photos, document photos, card photos, and time-lapse photos.
  • Photos: To select pet images and, when you choose, save cards, recaps, or time-lapse media to your Photos library.
  • Location: To measure and display a route after you start a walk. If background location is allowed, route collection may continue while that walk remains active. Location is not used for advertising.
  • Notifications: To schedule local reminders for daily moments, care plans, health tasks, time-lapse prompts, and available recaps.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Disabling a permission may make the related feature unavailable but does not prevent you from using unrelated parts of Fuzami.

5. Widgets and on-device features

If you add a Fuzami widget, the app shares a limited summary with the widget through Apple's App Group storage on the same device. This may include the selected pet's name, species, current mood, recent moods, total moments, days together, a note, and local image references. This information is used only to display the widget.

Recap calculations, card rendering, and similar app features are performed by the app. We do not use your pet content to train generative artificial-intelligence models or to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Purchases and RevenueCat

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment-card information.

Fuzami uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore purchases, check subscription status, and provide access to Fuzami Pro. Fuzami does not provide RevenueCat with a personal Fuzami account because the app does not have its own account system. RevenueCat creates an app-specific anonymous customer identifier.

RevenueCat may process information such as the anonymous app customer identifier, Apple receipt and purchase history, subscription and entitlement status, app version, device type and operating-system version, locale and currency information, and technical information required to provide purchase and subscription services.

Fuzami does not intentionally send pet profiles, health records, photos, health documents, notes, or saved walk routes to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's Privacy Policy is available at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

7. Diagnostics

Apple may provide developers with aggregated performance or diagnostic information according to your Apple and device privacy settings. Fuzami does not operate a third-party behavioral analytics service and does not attach pet names, notes, photos, document contents, or route coordinates to its own diagnostic logging.

8. Promotional website and support email

The Fuzami promotional website is intended to operate without advertising, behavioral analytics, user accounts, contact forms, newsletters, or non-essential cookies.

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure may automatically process limited technical request information, such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, request time, and security logs, only to deliver and protect the website.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use that information only to respond to your request, investigate a problem, prevent abuse, or keep an appropriate support record. Please do not send sensitive health documents, microchip identifiers, precise routes, or unrelated personal information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

9. How information is used

Information is processed only as needed to:

  • Provide the app features you choose to use.
  • Store and synchronize your records.
  • Display widgets and schedule local reminders.
  • Generate cards, reports, recaps, time-lapses, and exports.
  • Track a walk after you start it.
  • Validate purchases and provide Fuzami Pro access.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

10. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information for cross-app advertising or advertising tracking.

Information may be processed by Apple, RevenueCat, and website/email infrastructure providers only for the services described above. Service providers process information under their own privacy and security obligations. We may also disclose limited information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect users and our rights.

11. Retention and deletion

App records remain on your device and, when iCloud synchronization is available, in your private iCloud database until you delete them or manage Fuzami's iCloud data. Deleting a record in Fuzami removes it from the app, and the deletion is intended to synchronize through CloudKit.

Uninstalling Fuzami removes device-only app files but may not remove information already stored in iCloud. If you want to remove the iCloud copy, delete your records in Fuzami while synchronization is available or manage Fuzami's data through your iCloud settings before uninstalling.

12. Your choices

You can edit or delete records inside Fuzami, change camera/photo/location permissions in system Settings, manage an App Store subscription in your Apple Account settings, and contact us with a privacy question. Because Fuzami does not operate a user-account database, most app content can only be controlled directly from your device or iCloud account.

13. Children

Fuzami is not directed to children who cannot legally agree to the data processing described in this policy. We do not knowingly create advertising profiles about children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child sent personal information to our support email without authorization, please contact us.

14. Security

Fuzami uses Apple platform protections, app-container isolation, protected local files, and private CloudKit storage. However, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Fuzami's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when changes are published.
