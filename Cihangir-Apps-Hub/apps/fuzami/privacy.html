Privacy Policy for Fuzami

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Fuzami is developed and operated by Cihangir Kankaya ("Fuzami," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Fuzami app for iPhone or iPad, visit the Fuzami promotional website, or contact us for support.

1. Our approach to privacy

Fuzami does not require you to create a Fuzami account. We do not operate a separate server that receives your pet profiles, photos, notes, health records, health documents, or saved walk routes.

Your active app records are stored on your device. If you choose to enable Smart Backup, Fuzami creates versioned recovery points in your private iCloud app data. Smart Backup is a backup-and-restore feature; it does not provide live synchronization of active records between devices.

Fuzami does not contain advertising, does not use your information for advertising tracking, and does not sell your personal information.

2. Information you choose to store in Fuzami

Depending on the features you use, Fuzami may process the following information on your device:

  • Pet profile information, such as a pet's name, species, breed, birth date, adoption date, profile photo, allergies, diet, ideal weight, and microchip identifier.
  • Daily moments, including photos, moods, notes, dates, and favorite status.
  • Health and care information, including vaccinations, parasite treatments, vet visits, medications, surgeries, grooming, symptoms, laboratory results, dental care, insurance records, veterinarian names, notes, care schedules, dosage information, medication instructions, stock information, and completion history.
  • Weight records.
  • Health documents and attachments, such as PDFs, images, text files, CSV files, and Word documents.
  • Walk information, including start and end times, duration, distance, speed, and precise route points collected while a walk is active.
  • Generated cards, recap information, and time-lapse entries.
  • App preferences, such as units, theme, reminder times, selected pet, and widget selection.

Please do not enter personal or sensitive information about another person unless you are authorized to do so and it is necessary for your use of Fuzami.

3. Local storage, photos, and Smart Backup

Fuzami uses protected local app storage for your active records and small image previews. Full-quality photos and videos remain in your Photos library and are not duplicated into Smart Backup.

If you enable Smart Backup and iCloud is available, Fuzami may upload versioned recovery points to the app's private iCloud database. Recovery points may include your structured records, routes, small photo previews, and health documents when storage permits. Your iCloud storage availability may affect whether a recovery point is complete.

For the current backup history, Fuzami keeps up to seven recovery points. It may also retain the latest recovery point from up to two recent previous histories for up to 30 days. These limits are designed to provide useful recovery options while keeping storage bounded.

Apple provides and controls iCloud and CloudKit. Your use of those services is also governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

4. Device permissions

Fuzami may request the following permissions only when you choose to use a related feature:

  • Camera: To take pet photos, document photos, card photos, and time-lapse photos.
  • Photos: To select pet images and, when you choose, save cards, recaps, or time-lapse media to your Photos library.
  • Location: To measure and display a route after you start a walk. If background location is allowed, route collection may continue while that walk remains active. Fuzami does not use location for advertising.
  • Notifications: To schedule local reminders for daily moments, care plans, health tasks, time-lapse prompts, and available recaps.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Disabling a permission may make the related feature unavailable but does not prevent you from using unrelated parts of Fuzami.

5. Widgets and on-device processing

If you add a Fuzami widget, the app shares a limited summary with the widget through Apple's App Group storage on the same device. This may include the selected pet's name, species, current mood, recent moods, total moments, days together, a note, and local image references. This information is used only to display the widget.

Recap calculations, card rendering, and similar features are performed by the app. We do not use your pet content to train generative artificial-intelligence models or to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Purchases and RevenueCat

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment-card information.

Fuzami uses RevenueCat to offer and manage Fuzami Pro, validate purchases, restore purchases, check subscription status, and provide access to paid features. Because Fuzami has no account system of its own, RevenueCat assigns an app-specific customer identifier.

RevenueCat may process information needed to provide its service, including the app-specific customer identifier, Apple receipt and purchase history, subscription and entitlement status, app version, device type, operating-system version, locale, currency information, IP address, and related technical information.

Fuzami does not intentionally send pet profiles, health records, photos, health documents, notes, or saved walk routes to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

7. Diagnostics

Depending on your Apple and device privacy settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated performance, crash, or diagnostic information. Fuzami does not use a third-party behavioral analytics service and does not intentionally attach pet names, notes, photos, document contents, or route coordinates to its diagnostic logging.

8. Promotional website

The Fuzami promotional website does not offer user accounts, contact forms, behavioral advertising, or newsletters. We do not intentionally set non-essential cookies on the website.

The website is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may automatically process limited technical request information, such as IP address, browser type, requested page, request time, and security logs, to deliver and protect the website.

The website currently loads font and icon resources from Google Fonts and the cdnjs content-delivery network. When your browser requests these resources, the relevant provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser information, requested resource, and request time. These providers handle that information under their own privacy policies.

  • Cloudflare privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • cdnjs privacy information: https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/

If these external resources are later hosted directly by Fuzami, this section should be updated accordingly.

9. Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request, investigate a problem, prevent abuse, and maintain an appropriate support record.

Please do not send health documents, microchip identifiers, precise routes, or unrelated personal information unless it is necessary to resolve your request. Email is provided through our email service provider and is subject to that provider's privacy and security practices.

10. Why information is processed

We process information only as needed to:

  • Provide the app and website features you choose to use.
  • Store your active records on your device.
  • Create and restore optional Smart Backup recovery points.
  • Display widgets and schedule local reminders.
  • Generate cards, reports, recaps, time-lapses, and exports.
  • Track a walk after you start it.
  • Validate purchases and provide Fuzami Pro access.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect the app and website, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing may be based on performing our agreement with you, your request or consent when you enable an optional feature, our legitimate interests in providing and protecting Fuzami, or compliance with a legal obligation. You may withdraw a permission or consent through the app or system settings where applicable. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

11. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-app advertising or advertising tracking.

Information may be processed by Apple, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, our email provider, and the font or content-delivery providers identified above, but only for the services described in this policy. These providers process information under their own terms, privacy policies, and security obligations.

We may disclose limited information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect users, animals, our services, or our legal rights.

Some service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Any such processing is subject to the provider's safeguards and applicable data-protection law.

12. Retention and deletion

Active app records remain on your device until you delete them in Fuzami or remove the app. Deleting a pet profile permanently deletes that pet and its associated moments, health records and documents, care plans, weights, walks, cards, and time-lapse entries from the active data on that device.

Because Smart Backup keeps versioned recovery points, an item removed from active app data may remain in an earlier recovery point until that recovery point expires or all Fuzami iCloud backup data is deleted. You can remove all Fuzami recovery points from your iCloud account by opening Settings > Backup & Restore > Delete iCloud Backup Data in Fuzami. This also turns off Smart Backup but does not delete the active data stored on your device.

Uninstalling Fuzami removes device-only app files but may not remove recovery points already stored in iCloud. Files you exported, content you saved to Photos, and copies you shared with other people or services must be deleted separately from those locations.

Purchase and subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own legal and operational requirements. Website technical logs are retained according to the settings and policies of the relevant infrastructure providers. Support communications are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain continuity, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations, and are then deleted or anonymized when appropriate.

13. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • View, edit, or delete active records inside Fuzami.
  • Turn Smart Backup on or off.
  • Delete all Fuzami iCloud backup data from Settings > Backup & Restore.
  • Export a backup file and, where available, export health information in PDF or CSV format.
  • Change camera, Photos, location, and notification permissions in system Settings.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account settings.
  • Contact us with a privacy question or request concerning information we control, such as a support message.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Some rights may be limited where we do not possess or control the requested information. Because Fuzami does not operate a user-account database, most app content can be controlled only from your device, exported backup copies, or iCloud account.

14. Children

Fuzami is intended for people who can lawfully agree to these privacy practices in their country or who use the app with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly use the app or website to collect personal information directly from children, create advertising profiles about children, or direct behavioral advertising to children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child sent personal information to our support address without authorization, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

15. Security

Fuzami uses Apple platform protections, app-container isolation, protected local files, and private CloudKit storage for enabled backups. We limit the information sent to subscription and website providers as described above. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device, Apple Account, iCloud account, exported files, and any content you choose to share.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Fuzami's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date when a revised policy is published. If a change materially affects how we handle information, we will provide additional notice where required by law.
